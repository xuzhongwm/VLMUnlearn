## 4. Official evaluation protocol (code facts)

- Released fine-tuned checkpoints on the HF Hub (org `MLLMMU`): `LLaVA_Vanilla` (llava-1.5-7b-hf architecture), `Idefics2_Vanilla`. None for Qwen2-VL / Qwen2.5-VL. Not used in this project.
- Reported (not measured here) in the MLLMU-Bench paper, Table 2: vanilla LLaVA-1.5-7B classification accuracy, modalities pooled, 5% forget: forget 51.70%, test 47.86%, retain 46.11%, real celebrity 51.80%.
- MC scoring is generative: greedy decoding, the first character of the reply must be one of the option letters, otherwise the item is counted wrong (`eval.py:376`, `eval.py:432`).
- Forget/retain/test MC prompts include few-shot demonstrations from 1 profile sampled with `random.sample(id_list, 1)` from the IDs of the set being evaluated (`eval.py:263-265`); the demo profile's own questions are skipped. `eval.py` contains no `random.seed` call. Celebrity MC is zero-shot (`eval.py:344`, `eval.py:398`).
- Fine-tuning/unlearning labels are the full sequence with only padding masked (`data_process/data_preprocess.py:373`).
- NPO default `--beta 0.4` (`baselines/NPO.py:299`) with a separately fine-tuned retain-only reference model (`--oracle_model_id`). GA_Diff iterates a full forget pass, then a full retain pass, per epoch (`baselines/GA_Difference.py:360-381`).
- Official MC questions whose `Correct_Answer` is not one of the option letters: 1 (profile 028 img: 'What type of pet does this person have?' -> 'None of the above').

### 5.4 Label spot check

Labels were reviewed against the question text (and correct answer) on random samples:

- Round 1 (`random.seed(42)`, 60 fictitious + 20 celebrity questions, rules before revision): 54/60 and 14/20 judged
  correct. Error types: no 'parents' category ("professions of X's parents", "parents of X"); "companion during
  activities" (a pet) labelled hobby; "skill in addition to their profession" labelled profession; capitalised job
  titles taken as parent names; celebrity film/role questions had no category. The rules were revised once in response.
- Round 2 (`random.seed(2026)`, a fresh sample, final rules): fictitious 58/60 correct at the coarse attribute level
  used for the overlap figures (57/60 fine-grained); errors: "physical stature" -> appearance (should be height),
  "where likely based, considering their profession" -> profession (should be residence), "Who is X's mother? ->
  Realtor" -> mother_name (should be occupation; coarse label correct). Celebrities 29/30 (error: "type of vehicle
  known for riding" -> works_achievements).

The overlap percentages in §5 therefore carry label noise of roughly a few percent of questions.
